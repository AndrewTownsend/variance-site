Variance

A local-first poker session tracker.

Variance is a simple journal for the poker sessions you play. Track your buy-ins, cash-outs, locations, and notes, and let the app turn them into the statistics that actually matter. Everything stays on your device — no accounts, no servers, no cloud sync.
